How it started

A birthday, and tickets to London.

Genny gave me the camera for my birthday in November 2024, and tickets to London to go with it. I'd always wanted to go to the UK. That's the whole origin story — I didn't come to this from anywhere serious.

What I shoot with

Sony ZV

Does most of the work. The birthday camera.

TTArtisan AF 27mm

Autofocus, cheap, sharp enough that I stopped thinking about it.

Viltrox 28mm F4.5 pancake

Flat enough that the camera stays something I'll actually carry.

The kit lens

Came with the camera. Still earns its place some days.

A cheap Snapic

Where most of my film goes now. It is not a serious camera and I like it anyway.

Kodak UltraMax 400

36 exposures, nothing exotic. It forgives me being wrong about the light.

Olympus XA2 — retired

The aperture misbehaves. Unless the light is perfect and the batteries are fresh it hands back a blurry frame, so it sits on the shelf.

One thing to check: a ZV‑1 has a fixed lens, so the TTArtisan and the Viltrox point at a ZV‑E10 instead. Tell me which body it is and I'll put the right name on it.

A small card

Not much room, on purpose.

I keep very little storage in the ZV deliberately. With room for a few hundred frames instead of a few thousand, I have to decide a shot is worth taking before I take it. It's the one rule I've given myself and it's done more for my photos than any lens.
