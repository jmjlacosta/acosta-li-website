Reading list

It started with a weekend and about a book a day.

For our third anniversary, in March 2025, Genny and I spent a weekend at a getaway house. I went through roughly a book a day of the Bobiverse and have not really stopped since.

This isn't a shelf of favourites. It's what I've actually read, and it keeps growing. I'm writing something about each one as I go.

What I'm writing

Series A

A roguelike LitRPG about raising a round. The hero levels up and you get to see the numbers. The dungeon is a startup. The loot is headcount.

I haven't decided whether it's a game or a book, and at the moment it's quietly becoming both.

It's here rather than anywhere else on the site because it came straight out of this list. Read enough of the genre and you start keeping notes on what you'd do differently.

Draft. This is the part I know least about — tell me what Series A actually is and I'll rewrite it.
